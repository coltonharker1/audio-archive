Collection

The Cable Man

Found at a garage sale in Joliet, Illinois, October 2023
6 reels

The box was under a card table with a crimping tool and a coffee tin full of cable ends. On the lid someone had written “keep the short ones.” The shortest thing in the box is a station break that lasts just long enough for three voices to sing the name of a town.

There are three endings to that tune. On the first, a bass player lets the last note ring. On the second, somebody puts a hand on the strings. The third ends in the middle of the word “Joliet.” All three were wound onto one reel, with a little white leader between them.

I think these belonged to someone who worked on the local cable system. The line checks give me that impression. What I keep coming back to is the rain reel. After the gutter stops rattling, whoever held the microphone stayed out there for another minute.
