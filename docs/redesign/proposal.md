# Remanence for Mac

Follow RA-019, *Station break, three endings*, from the tape machine to its site entry. Its box label, collection and side stay visible as the work changes. The six reels, finding details and collection order come from the earlier *The Cable Man* contact-sheet mockup. Capture readings, track timings and site states are illustrative snapshots at different points in the work.

## What changes

- **Give the Shelf a memory.** Today the selected tape panel offers four destinations with equal weight. Put the last working position above the finding list. “Resume Side A” restores the track, playhead and zoom. Keep the existing work filters, file drop, backup import and transcript search. A search hit still opens Read Along at the matching moment.
- **Start with the box and finish with the side.** Before capture, choose the machine, input, channels, speed and collection, check levels, and show the prospective RA number. Record assigns it. During capture, the meter, elapsed time and assigned label take priority. Stop leads directly to the saved side, with Review, record the next side, re-record and return to Shelf together. Processing continues in the background.
- **Keep the passage through a mode change.** Today Review and Edit have separate headers and navigation. Give the tape one header and four tabs. Review places boundaries and trims a whole side. “Edit selected track” takes Side A at 0:42 directly to track 02 at 0:08. Returning restores the side view. Keep audible scrubbing, quiet-point splitting, marker undo, Hear the Join, track adjustments, append confirmation and side management. Read Along keeps line correction, notes and transcript comparison.
- **Show what each order changes.** The collection editor pairs the box essay with its contact sheet and lets the owner arrange the public reading order below. The Catalog moves from a shelf-only toolbar symbol to a named sidebar destination. It stages RA changes in a separate column, shows both affected box labels and confirms the batch before applying. Each tape keeps its number when moved within a collection.
- **Name the publication decision.** In Site Post, rename “Looks Good” to “Approve for push.” The approved state offers “Create unpublished draft.” Once sent, the inspector offers Publish and identifies who can see the reel. Keep save, progress, cancellation, retry, update, refresh, unpublish and removal in that context, including conflict feedback when the site copy has changed. Site Posts retains the cross-library listing and Bring In. A collection has its own draft and publication controls.

## Family resemblance

Use the site’s warm paper (`#faf5ee`) and charcoal ink (`#211d19`). Its EB Garamond voice carries into titles, track names, captions and essays through local Iowan Old Style, Palatino and Georgia. The Shelf takes its object-and-caption pairing from the home page and its aligned number, title and duration columns from the recordings index. The collection uses the site’s three-across contact sheet. Native window chrome, system sans controls, tabular timecodes and keyboard hints belong to the workbench. Muted rust marks a recording or edit position.
